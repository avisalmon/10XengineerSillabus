The 10X Engineer

A semester of engineering practice with AI agents

Not a replacement for the engineer, a tool the engineer manages. The course teaches you to work as a first line manager of AI agents: define a task, break it down, give the agent the right context, check the result and take responsibility for it.

Most of the work happens at the keyboard. Each topic opens with a short lecture and continues as practice. Alongside the practice we cover the theory, from classical machine learning to transformers, so that working with the tools rests on an understanding of what happens inside them. At the end of the semester each participant presents a working project in their own professional field.
